@@ phantom-clock
Optical phase space becomes a memory medium. Two rays can share a position and still differ in direction and timing, so collapsing them manufactures errors. This essay keeps phase as a first-class coordinate and documents the ellipsoid inside the clock.

@@ eigen-attention
Attention reframed as constrained spectral regression: tokens are eigenvectors under a task-induced operator, and the softmax is solved for rather than assumed. The repository extends the theorem, adds controlled experiments, and ships reproducible checks that show where the approximation holds.

@@ persistent-discovery-index
A microscope for agent behaviour. PDI reads pi, OpenAI, LangSmith and canonical traces to locate the exact boundary where useful discovery ends and wasteful exploration begins, and report that boundary at behavioural resolution instead of returning one blunt aggregate score.

@@ behavioral-quotients
Two computational histories may be equivalent when they produce the same relevant behaviour. This paper states the equivalence relation explicitly, derives which observations must be retained for it to hold, and presents the quotient as a design principle for computation.

@@ agent-guardrail
Policy enforcement and permission testing for AI agents. Mock enterprise APIs simulate synthetic user consent, and requests are blocked before deployment when an agent's authority is not actually established, so abstraction never erases the security-relevant distinctions between any two callers.

@@ FUTCache
A bounded-memory sufficient-state representation of explored geometry. FUTCache merges visited states whenever merging loses nothing a later decision needs, scaling to a million production traces while the cache itself stays fixed in size, and released three times in summer 2026.

@@ novelty-geometry
Traversal, ordered novelty, memory and asymptotic completion in metric and hyperbolic spaces. The framework formalizes which directions an agent has genuinely covered, separating reached territory from a reworded revisit of ground explored, and shows how completion behaves as space grows.

@@ tensor-to-tenant
A hundred-and-eight-week apprenticeship in AI systems engineering, spanning mathematics, ML systems, LLMs, distributed systems and design. Built as a static site with deploy automation, analytics integration, pacing budgets and a GPU redirect ladder, and published weekly for two full years.

@@ reify
A declarative SDK and compiler for constraint optimization and decision-intelligence engines. Constraints are declared rather than hand-encoded, and the compiler decides how much structure can be discarded while every remaining degree of freedom still changes the decision that comes out.

@@ docs

@@ NoetherLeap
Constraint-aware optimization that pairs physics-informed neural networks with Bayesian search and symmetry discovery. @@ axiom-synergy

@@ authorization-lattice
Two agents may request the same action while holding entirely different permissions. Ignoring that distinction produces a security failure, so the work orders authority in a lattice and still keeps ownership attached to objects that may have none at all.

@@ NitroSAT
