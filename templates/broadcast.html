Why an index should not list every episode

Fifty-one long-form episodes and ten short ones is not a bibliography, it is a second research programme in prose. The index therefore keeps one entry per show, and this page clusters the episodes into five arcs — the same five questions the rest of the index is organised around.

Read in order, the arcs trace the lineage: arithmetic structure first, then the invariants and operators that make it usable, then geometry and complexity, then information and intelligence, and finally the physics the whole thing rests on. Each episode keeps its forty-word summary, so the argument is still readable without pressing play.
